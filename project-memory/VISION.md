# VISION

## Philosophy
The project is inspired by positive psychology, connection, contribution, belonging, and collective wellbeing. Its website identity is "You Matter. You matter. We matter. What we do matters." The book may retain the title "You Matter. But this isn’t about you."

## Audience
The audience is anyone interested in small daily practices that contribute to personal and collective wellbeing.

## Tone
The website should feel warm, thoughtful, welcoming, and human, rather than corporate or overly clinical.

## Goals
- Create a space for small daily practices that contribute to personal and collective wellbeing.
- Foster connection and contribution through shared experiences.
- Make the collective effect of individual actions visible.

## Known Creative Principles
- Focus on small, repeatable actions.
- Emphasize connection and contribution.
- Make the collective effect of individual actions visible.
- Keep the tone warm, thoughtful, welcoming, and human.
