The idea

Wellbeing is personal. It is also collective.

Positive psychology studies the conditions and practices that can help people and communities flourish. These adventures translate three research areas into tiny, repeatable actions—then make their collective effect visible. This is an invitation to experiment, not a promise to fix your life.
